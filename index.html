About SacroFarms and Agro Services

SacroFarms and Agro Services is a leading agricultural manufacturing company strategically located in Ikpoba-Okha Local Government Area of Edo State. We are dedicated to transforming Nigeria's agricultural sector through quality manufacturing, processing, and value-added services.

Our state-of-the-art facility at KM4 Utesi-Obagie Road, off Benin-Abraka Highway, serves as a hub for agricultural innovation and excellence. We work closely with local farmers and agricultural stakeholders to ensure sustainable practices and superior product quality.

With a commitment to food security and agricultural advancement, SacroFarms combines traditional farming wisdom with modern manufacturing capabilities to meet the evolving needs of Nigeria's agricultural market.

Our Mission: To provide exceptional agricultural manufacturing services that enhance productivity, ensure quality, and contribute to Nigeria's food security and economic growth.

Our Agricultural Manufacturing Services

Agricultural Processing

We provide comprehensive processing services for various agricultural products, utilizing modern equipment and quality control measures to ensure optimal product standards and shelf life.

Crop Value Addition

Our value-added services transform raw agricultural produce into market-ready products, increasing profitability for farmers and ensuring consistent quality for consumers.

Agro Product Manufacturing

We manufacture a range of agricultural products designed to meet local and regional market demands, maintaining the highest standards of quality and safety.

Farm Input Supply

SacroFarms supplies quality agricultural inputs including seeds, fertilizers, and farming equipment to support productive and sustainable farming practices.

Agricultural Consultation

Our experienced team provides expert consultation services on agricultural best practices, crop management, and modern farming techniques to enhance productivity.

Storage and Logistics

We offer secure storage facilities and efficient logistics solutions for agricultural products, ensuring proper handling from farm to market.
